Meet the founders.

Girls for Knowledge is shaped by a commitment to education, community, and practical opportunities for girls to keep learning.

Founder, Documentry Producer, and Photographer

Zalfa Mohammadi

Zalfa Mohammadi is an Afghan education advocate, documentary photographer, and emerging filmmaker committed to defending the right of Afghan girls and young women to education. As the founder of Girls for Knowledge, a nonprofit organization, she works to expand educational opportunities, build practical skills, and nurture creativity among Afghan girls and women, empowering them to shape their own futures. Her organization also supports orphanages and leads charitable programs for those in need. Her humanitarian work is closely intertwined with her practice as a documentary photographer and filmmaker. Through images and film, she tells underrepresented stories and brings issues of gender equality, displacement, resilience, and human rights to an international audience, using art as a means of expressing the truth and carrying the voices of Afghan girls beyond borders.Guided by the conviction that education and art together are powerful tools for lasting change, Zalfa continues to open doors for Afghan girls, advocate for their rights, and ensure that their voices are heard around the world.

Co-Founder & Program Manager

Madina Masomi

I am Madina Masomi the program manager and co-founder at Girls for Knowledge. In addition, I have the experience of teaching English Language for those girls who were banned by Taliban to get education in Afghanistan for more than four years. We are providing online or in person classes for students to improve their skills.

Co-Founder & English Department Manager

Wahida Jamal

I am an English teacher and a member of the Girls for Knowledge team. I contribute to the organization by teaching English and conducting interviews for students applying to our English and Duolingo classes. Through my role, I support students in finding suitable learning opportunities and contribute to the educational goals of Girls for Knowledge

Co-Founder & Student Registrar

Fatima Rezaei

I am Fatima Rezaie, a Co-founder at Girls for Knowledge, where I coordinate student registration and admissions, prepare registration forms, place students in appropriate classes, and report on meetings and program activities. In addition, I work as the MHPSS Manager, Math Teacher, and English Language Teacher. Through Girls for Knowledge, we provide online and in-person classes to help girls continue their education, improve their academic skills, and build confidence for their future.
