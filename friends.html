International Collaboration: Nobel × Girls for Knowledge

Online Collaborative Leadership Workshop | December 2, 2025

Girls for Knowledge collaborated with Nobel, a global social learning community focused on youth learning and leadership, to organize an online collaborative leadership workshop featuring Andrew Sachs and Zalfa Mohammadi, Founder of Girls for Knowledge.Held via Google Meet, the workshop focused on youth leadership, collaborative learning, personal development, and the importance of equipping young people with the skills and knowledge needed to become future changemakers.This collaboration reflects Girls for Knowledge’s commitment to expanding educational opportunities, strengthening youth leadership, and building meaningful connections with international learning communities.

Looking Ahead

Building on this collaboration, Girls for Knowledge looks forward to organizing further joint programs, workshops, and educational initiatives with Nobel and its collaborators. These future activities aim to create more opportunities for young people to learn, develop leadership skills, exchange ideas, and contribute positively to their communities.
